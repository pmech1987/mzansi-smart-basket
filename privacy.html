2. Information stored on your device

The current app stores basket items, shopping preferences, province selection, favourites, settings and locally entered loyalty-card numbers in browser/device storage (localStorage). This information is intended to remain on your device in the current version. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to view it.

3. Location

Location is optional. When you tap the location button, the app asks the operating system or browser for the device's current coordinates and uses them in memory to estimate your South African province. The current app code does not save the latitude/longitude or send those coordinates to the app's price-feed server. You can choose a province manually instead.

4. Network requests and service providers

The app downloads its price feed from the configured feed URL and may open external retailer or catalogue websites when you choose those links. The website host, content-delivery provider, repository host and external websites may process technical information such as IP address, device/browser details and request logs under their own privacy terms. The operator must list the actual hosting, analytics, advertising and affiliate providers here before enabling them.

5. Offer-source processing

The operator may process retailer offer documents in a private back-office workflow to extract candidate product and price fields. In the current release, uploading original PDF/image documents to the Gemini API is disabled by default and must only be enabled for sources that the operator is authorised to share with that processor for the intended purpose. Source documents are not intended to be displayed in the public app. Before enabling this workflow, the operator must verify the applicable provider terms, data processing arrangements, retention settings and source permissions, and update this notice with accurate processor information.

6. Voice features

If you use the optional voice-search feature, the app may use the speech-recognition service provided by your browser, operating system or device. Depending on that provider and your settings, audio or speech data may be processed by that provider under its own terms. The current app does not intentionally upload voice recordings to the Mzansi Smart Basket price-feed server. The operator should verify the exact Android/browser behaviour before launch and update this notice if a separate speech service or provider is configured.

7. Accounts, analytics and advertising

The current version does not require an account and does not intentionally include an in-app analytics or advertising SDK. This must be revised before adding analytics, ad networks, affiliate tracking, accounts, push notifications or cloud-synchronised shopping lists. Such additions may involve further personal-information processing and disclosures.

8. Your choices and rights

You can use the manual province selector instead of granting location permission. You can clear app data or remove locally stored information through your device/browser settings. Under POPIA, individuals may have rights to be informed about collection, access and correction, objection, deletion in appropriate circumstances and complaint to the Information Regulator. Contact the responsible party using the details above.

9. Security and retention

Local information remains on the device until it is cleared by the user, browser or operating system. The operator should maintain access controls, protect repository and hosting credentials, and publish a breach-response contact process before enabling accounts or server-side information storage.

10. Children

The app is a general shopping tool and is not designed to collect children's personal information. Do not enter information about a child.
