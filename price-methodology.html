Price Sources & Methodology

Last updated: 25 November 2026.

What the app compares

The app is designed to compare factual offer details such as product name, pack size, retailer, advertised price, loyalty price, offer dates and applicable region. It should compare like-for-like products and show when a loyalty card, multi-buy or regional condition applies.

Source status

Demo data is illustrative only and is not a live offer. Unreviewed feed means data has been loaded or extracted but has not been checked by a person against its source. Human-reviewed feed means an operator has checked the listed offers against the source material available to them; it does not guarantee stock availability at every branch.

How offer data is collected

Offer fields are intended to be independently recorded or extracted from sources that the operator is entitled to access and process for the intended purpose. OCR/AI extraction creates candidates only; it does not grant copyright permission, confirm that a website permits automated access, or prove that an offer is accurate. Remote downloads are disabled by default and require a documented source approval. The app aims to publish its own structured factual offer records, not reproduce a retailer or catalogue publisher’s original flyer pages, product photos, logos, page design or substantial curated database.

Corrections

Before a price feed is marked human-reviewed, the operator should check product identity, pack size, normal and loyalty prices, multi-buy conditions, region, start/end dates and source URL. If the offer cannot be checked, it should not be published as a verified live offer.

Retailer material

Retailer names identify the source store only and do not imply endorsement. A source link and attribution are not a substitute for permission where permission is required. The app aims to publish independently structured offer facts and source links, not copy complete pamphlets, protected photographs, logos, layouts or a substantial third-party compilation without permission or another valid legal basis.
